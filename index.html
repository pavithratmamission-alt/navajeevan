Navajeevan

Staff app for Navajeevan Social Work Centre

The Navajeevan app helps our directors and staff run the centre's work:

  • Directors monitor centre activities, staff attendance, facility bookings and training programmes, and add staff members.
  • Staff see their assigned activities, stamp field attendance with GPS, and submit activity reports.

The app is for Navajeevan staff only. Accounts are created by a director, and staff sign in with their Google account or email.
